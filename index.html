With myHealth you can track…

Mood & Wellness

Log how you feel each day, track your energy levels, and spot patterns between mood, stress and lifestyle.

Activity Tracking

Track movement, walks and workouts to stay motivated each day. See how staying active supports your overall wellbeing and mood.

Medication

Keep all your pills, doses, and schedules in one place with clear reminders so you never miss a dose.

Heart Health

Record heart rate and blood pressure, see how your numbers vary day to day, and notice patterns that may affect your wellbeing. myHealth keeps your readings organised so you can share them with your doctor whenever you need.
